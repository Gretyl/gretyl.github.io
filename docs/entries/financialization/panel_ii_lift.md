## From: inflection card ② — 2008–10, "GFC: a pause, not a break"

**Current (~55 words):**
> The mark-to-market view (DFA) registered the equity crash as a temporary
> reduction in top-1% concentration (29.5% → 27.3%). The capitalized-income
> view (PSZ) saw essentially no movement. The crisis tests what we mean by
> wealth — but neither methodology shows a structural break. By 2013 every
> line is rising again.

**Lifted argument:**
- The crisis tests *what we mean by wealth*: DFA (mark-to-market) registers
  a dip (29.5% → 27.3%); PSZ (capitalized-income) sees no movement
- Neither methodology shows a structural break

**Proposed in-page replacement (~25 words):**
> DFA registers a temporary dip (29.5% → 27.3%); PSZ sees no movement.
> Neither methodology shows a structural break. By 2013 every line rises again.
